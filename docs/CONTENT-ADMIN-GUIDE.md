# SRKR Website — Content Management Guide

You can update the **Upcoming Events** and **News & Updates** shown on the SRKR
website (srkrec.ac.in). No coding or developer needed.

## 1. Log in
- Open **https://admin.srkrec.ac.in/admin**
- Sign in with your **email and password** (shared with you separately)
- Please **change your password** after your first login (top-right → your account)

## 2. What you manage
- **Upcoming Events** — workshops, FDPs, conferences, seminars
- **News & Updates** — achievements, announcements, notices
- Department editors manage **their own department only**. Principal/Admin manage everything.

## 3. Add an Event
1. Click **Upcoming Events → Create New**
2. Fill in: **Title**, **Department** (auto-set for department logins), **Date**,
   **Venue**, **Description**, optional **Registration link**, optional **Image / Attachment**
3. Set **Status = Published**
4. Click **Save**

## 4. Add a News item
1. Click **News & Updates → Create New**
2. Fill in: **Title**, **Date**, **Category** (District-level / Department-level),
   optional **Links**, optional **Image / Attachment**
3. Set **Status = Published**
4. Click **Save**

## 5. Publish, hide, edit, delete
- **Published** = shows on the website.  **Draft** = hidden (use while preparing).
- To hide something later: open it → set **Status = Draft** → **Save**.
- To edit: open it → make changes → **Save**.  To remove: open it → **Delete**.

## 6. When do changes appear?
- The website updates **automatically, about 1–2 minutes** after you publish, hide,
  edit, or delete. Nothing else to do — no need to contact the developer.

## 7. Tips
- Keep titles clear and short.
- Use **Draft** while preparing; switch to **Published** when ready.
- Don't share your login — each person has their own account.
