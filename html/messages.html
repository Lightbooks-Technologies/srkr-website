Message

President's Message

S. Prasad Raju
President

A Vision Rooted in Purpose

SRKR Engineering College was founded by Sri Sagi Rama Krishnam Raju with the mission to empower society through holistic, multidisciplinary education. Guided by his vision, we continue to blend Humanities and STEM to create meaningful learning experiences.

Progressing with Purpose

From strong academic linkages to forward-thinking initiatives, SRKREC has emerged as a premier institution in India. We continually evolve to meet global challenges through innovation, excellence, and a dedicated academic ecosystem.

Empowering Future Leaders

Our students are nurtured to be responsible global citizens. With a focus on values, creativity, and character, SRKREC remains committed to shaping future-ready individuals who lead with integrity and purpose. We take pride in fostering not only intellect but also empathy. Together, we build a future shaped by vision, courage, and care.

Dr. M.Jagapathi Raju
Director

A Launchpad for Lifelong Success

SRKR Engineering College has been shaping bright futures for over four decades. Located in the culturally rich Godavari region, it offers 12 B.Tech. programs, strong placement records, and startup support to help students turn ambition into achievement.

Driving Innovation with Purpose

With 18 Centres of Excellence and global research ties, SRKR fosters innovation across STEM and humanities. Cutting-edge labs, EU-India projects, and a skilled faculty ensure students are equipped for real-world impact and lifelong learning.

Nurturing Well-Rounded Individuals

Beyond academics, SRKR encourages personal growth with top-tier sports, wellness, and activity spaces. A 24/7 learning culture helps students discover their strengths, cultivate discipline, and grow into capable, ethical professionals. Here, your ambitions are matched with opportunity. The SRKR experience is one of transformation, inclusion, and limitless potential.

Prof. K V Murali Krishnam Raju
Principal

A Warm Welcome to SRKR Engineering College

It is with great pride and excitement that I welcome all to S R K R Engineering College. Our institution is known for academic strength, professional integrity, and a student-centric ethos that inspires success both in and out of the classroom.

Honoring a Legacy, Inspiring the Future

Founded in 1980, SRKREC has been a cornerstone of technical education in Andhra Pradesh. With committed faculty, a legacy of excellence, and an eye toward the future, we offer an environment where talent thrives and innovation blossoms.

Empowering Through Diversity and Opportunity

We believe in learning that empowers. By embracing diversity, promoting active participation, and supporting individual growth, we prepare our students not just for jobs, but for meaningful careers that make a difference in the world.

SRKR Engineering College, Bhimavaram – empowering rural talent with global exposure since 1980. Accredited, industry-aligned, and future-focused.
